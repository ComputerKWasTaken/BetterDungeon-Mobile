# V2.1 Phase 4 Device Gate

Run this checklist on at least one phone before closing the V2.1 Mobile UI gate.
Repeat the layout checks on a tablet or resizable emulator when available.

## Launcher and lifecycle

- Open an adventure and confirm the compass launcher appears only after the gameplay UI is present.
- Drag the launcher to each edge and confirm it remains fully visible.
- Reload the adventure and confirm the saved launcher position is restored.
- Navigate between two adventures and confirm the previous transcript is not shown while the new session loads.
- Leave gameplay and confirm the launcher and sheet are removed without duplicate UI after returning.

## Full-screen sheet and IME

- Tap the launcher and confirm Navigator fills the visual viewport on both phone and tablet sizes.
- Confirm opening the sheet does not focus the composer or automatically open the keyboard.
- Tap the composer and confirm the keyboard does not cover the input, Send, or Stop controls.
- Enter text through a composing IME and confirm Enter does not send before composition finishes.
- Rotate the device and resize the keyboard; confirm the header, transcript, and composer stay inside the visible viewport.
- Confirm system bars, display cutouts, and gesture navigation do not cover interactive controls.
- Confirm proposal comparisons stack vertically on a narrow phone and all interactive controls have comfortable touch targets.

## Back priority

- Open BetterDungeon Settings and press Back; confirm Settings closes first.
- Open Navigator and press Back; confirm Navigator closes without changing AI Dungeon history.
- With both overlays closed, press Back and confirm normal WebView navigation occurs.
- Press Back repeatedly while Navigator is closing and confirm no double navigation or app exit occurs.

## Settings synchronization

- Disable and re-enable Navigator in BetterDungeon Settings; confirm the launcher is removed and restored without reloading.
- Toggle Read-only mode while Navigator is open; confirm the badge and available actions refresh immediately.
- Close and reopen Settings and confirm the Read-only toggle persists.
- Disable Navigator, change Read-only mode, re-enable Navigator, and confirm the new session reads the persisted value.

## Conversation regression

- Send a grounded question and confirm streamed text, thinking state, and Story Card tool activity render correctly.
- Stop an active response and confirm no late completion appears.
- Clear the conversation and confirm persisted history is removed for that adventure.
- Background and foreground the app with Navigator open and confirm the sheet remains usable.
- Inspect Logcat for uncaught JavaScript, bridge, IME, or Back-navigation errors.

Mutation application remains a Phase 5 gate. Phase 4 must remain fail-closed when
the mutation service is absent.
