# V2.1 Phase 5 Device Gate

Run this checklist in a disposable test adventure before using Navigator writes
in an important adventure. Keep a second AI Dungeon client or browser available
for the conflict cases.

## Approval boundary

- Turn Navigator Read-only off and ask it to change one Plot Component. Confirm a proposal card appears and the adventure is unchanged before tapping **Apply**.
- Confirm the card shows the complete before/after comparison and a clear target name.
- Tap **Apply** once. Confirm the control disables immediately, only one write occurs, and the card reaches **Applied** only after AI Dungeon returns the saved value.
- Rapidly tap **Apply** or rotate/background the app during application. Confirm the proposal is still applied no more than once.
- Create another proposal, tap **Reject**, then confirm it cannot later be applied.
- Create a deletion proposal and confirm the permanent-deletion warning is visible before approval.

## Read-only and adventure identity

- Enable Read-only while Navigator is open. Confirm the badge updates immediately and proposal tools are unavailable on the next turn.
- With a pending proposal visible, enable Read-only and tap **Apply**. Confirm the apply fails closed and the adventure remains unchanged.
- Prepare a proposal in adventure A, switch to adventure B before applying, and confirm no write reaches either adventure.
- Reload BetterDungeon while a proposal is pending. Confirm the old proposal is not restored as actionable.

## Conflict checks

- Prepare a Plot Essentials or Author's Note proposal, edit that same field from the other client, then tap **Apply**. Confirm Navigator reports a conflict and preserves the external edit.
- Prepare a Story Card update, modify that card from the other client, then tap **Apply**. Confirm Navigator reports a conflict without overwriting the card.
- Repeat with a Story Card deletion after editing the target card. Confirm the edited card remains present.

## Verified write coverage

- Apply an AI Instructions change and confirm the custom instructions value is exact after reloading the adventure.
- Apply Plot Essentials, Author's Note, Story Summary, and Third Person changes; test both non-empty values and removal where applicable.
- Create a Story Card and verify Type, Name, Triggers, Entry, and Notes exactly match the proposal after reload.
- Update each Story Card field, including clearing a field, and confirm unrelated fields remain unchanged.
- Delete a disposable Story Card and confirm it remains absent after reload.
- Temporarily interrupt connectivity immediately after tapping **Apply**. Confirm Navigator never reports **Applied** unless its post-write read-back succeeds.

## Regression and diagnostics

- Confirm grounded questions and read-only Story Card tools still work before and after an applied mutation.
- Stop an active model response, then apply a separate pending proposal and confirm the two cancellation paths do not interfere.
- Close Navigator during an apply and reopen it. Confirm there is no duplicate write and no stale actionable proposal.
- Inspect Logcat and the WebView console for uncaught GraphQL, bridge, storage, or proposal-state errors.
